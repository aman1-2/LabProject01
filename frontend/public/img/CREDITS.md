including commercially, with no attribution required. They are credited here

say where it came from costs a great deal.

## The rule this file exists to enforce

**Every image was opened and looked at before it was put on a page, and its
`alt` text describes what is actually in the frame.**

That is not a formality. The first set of images in this directory was fetched
from photo IDs guessed from memory and used without being viewed: four of the
six turned out to be a hotel bedroom, a cup of loose pills, an orange pill
bottle, and an anatomical brain model — all shipped with confident captions
describing a laboratory. On a medical site, alt text that misdescribes the
image is both an accessibility failure and a false statement about a picture a
patient is being shown. Do not add a file here without viewing it.

## What these images are not

None of them were taken at a PathCare collection or at a partner lab. They are
**illustrative**. Captions on the site therefore describe what the service
does, never what a particular picture shows, and no image is presented as our
staff, our equipment or our premises.

Photography from a competitor's site is licensed to them; copying it would be
infringement however well it fitted. If these are ever replaced with real
photographs of our own collections, written consent is required before any
identifiable person appears.
